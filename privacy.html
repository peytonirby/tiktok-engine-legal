Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

TikTok Content Engine is a privately operated application used to prepare, manage, and upload video content to authorized social media accounts.

Information Used

The application may process account authorization information required to connect to supported third-party services such as TikTok.

This may include access tokens, account identifiers, video files, posting status information, and other information required to perform authorized content-management functions.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to authenticate authorized accounts, process video content, upload content, and manage application functionality.

Data Sharing

Personal information is not sold or rented. Information may be transmitted to third-party services only when necessary to provide requested functionality, such as uploading content to TikTok.

Data Storage

Application data may be stored locally on the user's computer. Authentication credentials and tokens should be stored securely and used only for authorized API access.

Contact

Questions regarding this privacy policy may be directed to the operator of the application.
